Privacy Policy — YouTube Video Generator

Operated by QH Essentials LLC
Effective date: November 19, 2026

What this application is

YouTube Video Generator is a personal software tool that runs locally on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, it is not offered to other users, and it operates no external servers that collect data.

Google account access

The tool signs in with Google OAuth, with the account owner's consent, to access the owner's own YouTube channel or channels. It uses that access only to upload videos the owner has prepared and to read the channel's analytics, on the owner's behalf.

What data is stored, and where

OAuth access tokens and any channel data the tool retrieves are stored only on the owner's own computer. No personal data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or transmitted to any server operated by QH Essentials LLC.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the tool from reaching the channel until the owner signs in again.
